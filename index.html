Ask us a question ↗
How does TwistLock™ work?+

After inserting the hoop post into its closure, you rotate the locking element to engage the mechanism. To remove the earring, rotate it back to the unlocked position. Follow the instructions supplied with your specific style.

Which hoop sizes are available?+

The EAR200 series is shown in four diameters: 20 mm, 24 mm, 28 mm, and 35 mm. Other TwistLock styles may also be available through Empire Casting House.

Can I inquire about metals or finishes?+

Yes. Send us your preferred size, metal, quantity, and any other specifications. Our team will confirm current options, availability, and pricing.

Can I order online today?+

This website is currently an inquiry experience, not a live checkout. Add items to your inquiry bag and send your request to Empire Casting House to receive purchasing information.
